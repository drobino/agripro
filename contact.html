02 • QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Who can use AgriPro Ghana's resources?

Our content and programs are open to farmers of any scale, agricultural students, cooperatives, and organizations interested in sustainable agriculture.

Is there a cost to get involved?

Most of our educational resources are free. Some smart farming pilot programs may have limited availability depending on funding and partner capacity.

Do I need special equipment to start?

No. Many of our recommended practices, from crop rotation to basic soil testing, require little to no special equipment to get started.

How can my organization partner with you?

Send us a message using the form above with details about your organization and goals, and our team will follow up to explore next steps.

Where do you currently operate?

We currently work with farming communities across several regions of Ghana, including Ashanti, Northern and Eastern regions, with plans to expand further.
